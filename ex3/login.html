Online shopping is a form of electronic commerce which allows consumers to buy goods or services directly from a seller over the Internet using a web browser or a mobile app. Consumers generally find a product of interest by visiting a retailer's website specifically. Online platforms offer exclusive deals, discounts, and a broader selection of products than most brick-and-mortar shops. With the ability to compare prices and read customer reviews, you can make better purchasing decisions. Plus, many online shops provide easy return policies and secure payment options.
